## Upstream inspiration

UI and developer experience in this project were inspired by and adapted from
Laravel's official Svelte starter kit.

- Upstream reference commit: `48aaf1712c9f6099f7c83a93119495553b8f9958`
- Upstream repository: [laravel/svelte-starter-kit](https://github.com/laravel/svelte-starter-kit)

Portions of layout, styling, and UX patterns may derive from that upstream work.
This community project reimplements the frontend as a **true SPA** using
**sv-router** and **Axios** with **Fortify** and **Sanctum** — without Inertia.
